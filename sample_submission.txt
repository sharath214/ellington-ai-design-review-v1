ELLINGTON DEMO PROJECT — SCHEMATIC DESIGN — ARCHITECTURE — V1

The proposed residential building comprises ground floor, podium and upper residential floors.
The design includes an external staircase connecting the podium landscape level to the ground floor.
Parking is provided within the podium with designated resident and visitor parking bays.
The façade concept uses glazing, stone finish and metal screening elements.
General building height and floor-level information is indicated in the architectural elevation package.

Note: Setback dimensions are not stated in this narrative extract and should be verified against the submitted drawings and applicable Development Control Regulations.
